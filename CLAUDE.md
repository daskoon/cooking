# Personal Keto Hub: Design Context

## Users
- **The Visionary & Partner**: Private use for meal planning and grocery shopping. 
- **Context**: Quick lookups while cooking or at the store. Low-light (kitchen) and high-glare (store) environments.

## Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Helpful, Direct, Personal.
- **Tone**: Premium but intimate. "Our Private Kitchen Labs."
- **3-Word Personality**: Impeccable, Effortless, Intimate.

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Deep emerald glassmorphism. High contrast for readability.
- **Theme**: Dark mode by default (easier on the eyes in the kitchen).
- **Anti-References**: Avoid "Generic Recipe Site" (no ads, no fluff, no SEO-bait text).

## Design Principles
1. **Macro-First**: Net Carbs and Sodium are the heroes. No buried data.
2. **Invisible Motion**: Animations must serve a purpose (feedback/state changes), never distract.
3. **One-Tap Sharing**: Specifically optimized for sending to partner's Messenger.
4. **Offline Resilience**: Must work 100% without signal.
